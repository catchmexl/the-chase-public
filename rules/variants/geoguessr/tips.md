## For Everyone

- **Think like your opponent** — anticipate what your opponents will do next and plan accordingly
- **Watch the clock** — know which phase is coming and prepare for it
- **Every activated Clue Point is gone** — once used by any team, that point is disabled for everyone for the rest of the game
## For Chasers

- **Communication is key** — share ideas and plans constantly via the messenger group and coordinate together!
- **Stay opportunistic** — every Chaser team can activate Clue Points when the moment is right
- **Time Freeze carefully** — use it when stopping the Escapers creates the strongest advantage
- **Use 360° Video to narrow the search** — compare visible landmarks, streets, and transport clues with the map
- **Split up** — divide across different areas to maximize coverage _(players within a team must stay together)_
- **Predict their moves** — study the metro map and anticipate where the Escapers are heading. Set traps at key intersections before they arrive
## For Escapers

- **Plan your Freeze activation** — activating it reveals your location to the Chasers, so choose the right moment
- **Have an escape route ready** before activating Freeze — the Chasers will know exactly where you were
- **Stay unpredictable** — avoid obvious routes and change your plan regularly
- **Prepare for the Closing In phase** — plan ahead for when you can no longer use public transport
