Clue Points are locations marked on your mode's map. Approach within 50 metres to activate an available point. Each point can be used once per round, shared across all players.

Freeze and Live Tracking share a cooldown for the activating side. After either effect ends, that side waits {{COOLDOWN}} minutes before using another Freeze or Live Tracking point or tracking power-up. Follow the app's pending-response and cooldown indicators.
